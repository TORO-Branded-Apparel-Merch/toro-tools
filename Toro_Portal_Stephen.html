Rep Portal User Guide
Reference this anytime without losing your work

What This Tool Is

Your rep portal is where you track quotes, manage order status, and submit for commission. Quotes come in from the Quote Builder as JSON files. Once submitted, management reviews them on the dashboard.

Logging In

Enter your password to access the portal. Your session stays active for 4 hours, after that you will need to log back in.

If you need a new password, click the Password button in the top right and follow the prompt to contact management.

Uploading a Quote

Drag and drop the JSON file exported from the Quote Builder into the drop zone at the top of your portal. The quote will appear in your Active Quotes section. You can upload multiple quotes.

To remove a quote, use the delete button on the quote card.

If you exported the quote from the Quote Builder and something looks off, go back to the Quote Builder, drag the same JSON in to reload it, make your changes, and export again. Then re-upload the new file here.

Active Quotes

All quotes currently in progress live here. Each card shows the client name, quote number, in-hands date, sell total, margin, and estimated commission.

Expand a quote card by clicking the arrow to see its status checkboxes. Use these to track where each order stands.

Quote ApprovedClient has signed off on the quote.
Order CompletedOrder has been delivered.
Paid by ClientPayment has been received.
Ready for PaymentMarks the quote ready to submit for commission and moves it to the submission queue. Uncheck this if you need to pull it back before submitting.

Submitting for Commission

When one or more quotes are marked Ready for Payment, a submission section appears above Active Quotes showing your totals and estimated commission.

You can submit quotes individually using the Submit button on each card, or submit everything in the queue at once using the Submit All button. Once submitted, quotes move to your Archived section and management can see them on the dashboard.

Use Submit All when you have multiple quotes ready at the same time. It sends everything in one shot and saves you from clicking through each one.

Archived / Submitted

Quotes that have been submitted for commission appear here and are locked from editing. A blue Submitted badge shows while commission is pending.

Once you receive your commission, click the Mark as Paid button on the quote card. The badge updates to a green Paid status.

Report

Click the Report button in the top right to export an Excel file of your quotes. You can filter by date range and sort by quote date, in-hands date, or commission period.

Feedback

Use the Feedback button to send a note to management directly from the portal. Use this for questions, issues, or anything you want them to see related to your quotes.
