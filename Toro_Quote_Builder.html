What This Tool Does

The Quote Builder walks you through building a complete decorated apparel and promo quote in 5 steps. When you are done, it exports a JSON file you can load into your rep portal to track commission and submit for payment, or into the PO Builder to generate vendor orders.

Use the tabs above to jump to any step. You can open this guide at any time without losing your work.

The quote number on Step 1 must match your Printavo quote number. Everything ties together on that number.

Step 1 -- Order Info

Quote Number Must match the quote number in Printavo exactly. This is a requirement, not a suggestion.
Rep, Client Name, Company Fill these in accurately. They carry through to exports and POs.
In-Hands Date The client's required date. Check "Firm" if this date cannot move. This shows on POs so vendors understand the urgency.
Commission Split Standard is the default for your own accounts. Select a Hand-Off rate if this is a client being handed to you by the house. Hand-Off rates tier up as the relationship grows: order 1 you receive 25% of the rep share, order 2 you get 50%, order 3 you get 75%, and from order 4 on you receive the full rep share. Your portal reflects your portion automatically.
Has AM Check this if an account manager is involved on the order.
Order Notes For your own reference. Capture anything you want to remember about this order later.
At the bottom of Step 1, next to the Start Over button, there is a collapsed drop area. Click the arrow to open it and drag in a previously exported JSON to pick up where you left off.

Step 2 -- Apparel

Click "Add apparel item" for each garment style in the order.

Garment Details Fill in type, vendor, item name, SKU, color name, and apparel color. The color name field (like "Black Frost" or "Navy") is for your reference only and has no effect on pricing. The apparel color selection (dark, black, light, white) is what determines whether a white base screen is applied.
White Base If you select a dark or black apparel color, the white base checkbox checks automatically. If you do not want a base screen charged for a specific reason, uncheck it manually. Light and white apparel color selections will not include a base screen option at all.
Size Groups and Pricing Groups Sizes are entered as rows within each garment. You can manually assign sizes to a group (Group A, Group B, etc.) when you need different net pricing across sizes, most commonly to apply 2X-4X upcharges separately from standard sizes. Once sizes share a group, you can apply net cost changes to the whole group at once using the group button that appears. Quantities and upcharges are always entered manually per size.
Invoice Grouping If this garment is part of a larger Printavo project that includes other items being invoiced and commissioned together, assign a matching invoice group name here. This tells the tool to treat those items as one project. You only need this when items are not already connected through a shared imprint. For most multi-style orders, shared imprints handle this automatically.
Misc Fees Use these to capture any additional costs tied to this order. Credit card fees are the most common use. You can enter a flat total for the whole project or a per-piece run charge. These roll into the loaded net cost on the pricing page.
Imprint Types Each garment supports multiple imprint locations. Click the imprint type (Screen, Embroidery, DTF, Heat press, Vinyl) to switch and fill in the required fields for each.
Sharing Imprints The share button only appears on a garment's imprint after a second garment exists in the order, either added manually or duplicated. It shows up on the first garment's imprint card, so scroll back up after adding your second garment to find it. If two or more styles are getting the same imprint, set it up on the first garment only, click the share button, and select which other garments it applies to. You can share across all sizes or select specific quantities. The tool combines all quantities into one run for pricing, giving you the better quantity break. Each garment can still have its own additional imprints that are not shared.
Deco Override If you need to price a decoration using a different rate than the standard grid (legacy pricing, a special deal, etc.), check the override toggle on that imprint and enter a flat per-item deco cost. The rest of the pricing math runs from that number as normal.

Step 3 -- Hard Goods

Click "Add hard good item" for each non-apparel product.

Fields Fill in vendor, SKU, description, decoration type, deco location, design name, imprint colors, and quantity.
Net Cost Per Item Your actual cost from the vendor.
Run Charge Per-piece decoration charge on top of the item cost.
Setup / Die Charge One-time setup fee. The tool spreads this across quantity when calculating the loaded net.
Invoice Grouping Same as on the apparel page. If this hard good is part of a Printavo project being commissioned with other items, give it the same invoice group name.

Step 4 -- Pricing

How the Pricing Grid Works Each garment shows rows broken out by size group. Every row displays the loaded net (blank cost plus deco plus any fees, per piece), your margin percentage, and the sell price per item. Edit either the margin or the sell price and the other updates automatically.
Project vs. Individual Pricing When garments share imprints or share an invoice group, the tool prices the decoration as one combined run across all items. This is how you get better quantity breaks on a multi-style order. Items not connected by either of those price independently.
Margin Flags If margin drops below 35%, the row flags in orange as a heads up. Below 30% it flags in red. Orders under 30% follow a different commission structure and will need manager awareness before submitting. You can still proceed and will still be paid. This is informational.
Absorbed Fees Any misc fee or line item where the sell price is set to $0 is absorbed into your cost. It raises your loaded net without adding to the sell price, which lowers your margin. Use intentionally.

Step 5 -- Summary and Export

Review your totals and line items. When everything looks right, click Export to save a JSON file to your computer. You can also download an internal order summary as a PDF using the button on this page.

The JSON file is what you drag into your rep portal to track the quote and submit for commission. It also loads into the PO Builder when you are ready to generate vendor orders.

Save the JSON file somewhere you can find it. It is the only way to reload the quote for editing. Drag it back into the Quote Builder at any time and export again when done.

Common Questions

Can I set different margins for different size groups?
Yes. Each row in the pricing grid is editable independently.
Can I edit a quote after exporting?
Yes. Drag the JSON back into the Quote Builder and it reloads the full quote. Export again when done.
Why is my imprint cost higher than expected?
Check for specialty ink charges, sleeve add-ons, or PMS color match fees. All of these add to the imprint cost. You can also use the deco override to enter a specific rate instead.
Do I need both shared imprints and invoice grouping?
Usually no. Shared imprints handle most multi-style orders automatically. Invoice grouping is for more complex Printavo projects where items need to be commissioned together but do not share a decoration.
Why is the white base option not showing for my garment?
If you selected a light or white apparel color, there is no base screen needed and the option does not appear.
The share imprint button is not showing up.
You need at least two garments in the order before the share option appears. Add or duplicate a second garment first, then scroll back to the first garment's imprint card.
